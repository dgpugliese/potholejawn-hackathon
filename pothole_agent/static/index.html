      How this route was chosen

          Markers are open resident reports of street defects from the last 12 months, from Philadelphia 311. They are not verified and some may be patched already. The smooth route treats every open report as a time penalty on its street; it is a plan, not a guarantee. Plan before you drive, not while driving.

          Ask anything about Philadelphia's 311 service requests. Answers come straight from the city's live data, with the queries shown.

          Checking the city's data

            Data: OpenDataPhilly. Routing: OSRM plus our own pothole-aware router on OpenStreetMap streets. Map: Esri, OpenStreetMap contributors.
